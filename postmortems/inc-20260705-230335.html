What broke

At 2026-07-05 23:03:33Z, the estate event stream reported that HTML or link validation for atlas-systems.uk had failed. Atlas Blackbox captured the rolling evidence window and sealed incident inc-20260705-230335 two seconds later.

The available incident record proves a validation failure was reported. It does not prove that the public site was unavailable to visitors, that a deployment failed, or that production content changed.

Impact

The automated validation path could not confirm the expected public-site contract. This reduced confidence in the state of atlas-systems.uk until the check could be investigated.

No visitor-impact measurement, failed production request, deployment rollback, or persistent service outage is present in the captured evidence.

Root cause

Root cause remains unconfirmed.

The evidence is sufficient to say that a validation check failed, but insufficient to distinguish among a content defect, a broken link, a transient network response, a stale deployment edge, or a defect in the validation job itself. Assigning one of those explanations would exceed the recorded evidence.

Corrective actions

  • Include the exact failed URL, check identifier, response status, and bounded validator output in future failure events.
  • Attach the candidate or deployed commit SHA when validation is associated with a deployment.
  • Record whether the failure occurred before a site push, during deployment verification, or against an already-live route.
  • Keep Blackbox incident records separate from confirmed outage claims unless visitor-facing evidence supports that conclusion.
  • Reopen this postmortem only if preserved logs or deployment evidence establish a narrower root cause.

Review conclusion

This postmortem is published as an unresolved evidence record. It documents what the estate observed, what it did not observe, and which missing diagnostics prevented a defensible root-cause conclusion.
